\datedsubsection{\textbf{SimpleTIR: End-to-End RL for Multi-Turn Tool-Integrated Reasoning}}{$2025.3$ -- $2025.9$}
We introduce SimpleTIR, a plug-and-play reinforcement learning algorithm that stabilizes multi-turn Tool-Integrated Reasoning (TIR) training. We identify that training instability in multi-turn TIR is primarily caused by distributional drift from external tool feedback, leading to catastrophic gradient norm explosions. SimpleTIR addresses this by filtering void turns -- turns yielding neither a code block nor a final answer -- from trajectories during policy updates. Starting from the Qwen2.5-7B base model without supervised fine-tuning, SimpleTIR elevates the AIME24 score from 22.1 to 50.5, achieving state-of-the-art performance. The model also discovers diverse reasoning patterns such as self-correction and cross-validation.
As the \textbf{corresponding author}, I guided the research direction, experimental design, and contributed to the key findings on training dynamics.

\datedsubsection{\textbf{SimpleRL-Zoo: Investigating and Taming Zero RL for Open Base Models}}{$2024.12$ -- $2025.3$}
We systematically investigate zero reinforcement learning -- applying RL directly on base models without prior supervised fine-tuning -- inspired by the success of DeepSeek-R1. By testing across 10 diverse open base models (Llama3, Mistral, Qwen2.5, etc.), we uncover key design strategies including format rewards and query difficulty control. Notably, we observe the first emergence of verification behavior (``aha moment'') in non-Qwen small models. Our open-source framework SimpleRL-Zoo provides a comprehensive toolkit for zero RL research.
As a \textbf{co-first author}, I co-led the research direction, experimental design, and contributed to the key findings on training dynamics.

\datedsubsection{\textbf{RegMix: Automated Data Mixture Optimization for Pre-training}}{$2024.2$ -- $2024.7$}
RegMix formulates pre-training data mixture selection as a regression problem: we train hundreds of small proxy models on diverse random mixtures, fit a regression model from mixture weights to target metrics, and extrapolate to identify the optimal mixture before launching the large run. With only 10\% of the compute of prior methods, RegMix discovers mixtures that outperform human expert selection and DoReMi, and the discovered mixtures transfer reliably from 1M-parameter proxies to billion-scale models. RegMix has been adopted in real production pre-training (e.g., the Sailor model family). As the \textbf{co-first author}, I proposed the original idea and led the project.
